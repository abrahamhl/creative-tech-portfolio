## Role filters

**Hard constraints:** work legally from Spain (remote with Spanish payroll/EOR/appropriate cross-border setup, or hybrid based in Spain), ES/EN working languages; basic Dutch is a cultural plus **not** fluent Dutch. No claimed engineering degree, 5 years of professional enterprise software deployment, PLC/SCADA credentials, or production-model ownership.

**Selection order:** ES + EN, customer-facing tech, websites/agency workflows, industry and SaaS onboarding, actionable employer advertisement. Remote-first preferred, Barcelona/Vigo hybrid considered conditional on location.

## Legal check before accepting a Dutch-employer contract while living in Spain
- A full-time permanent move to Spain is **not** automatically covered by the ordinary Netherlands–Spain cross-border telework special framework (which is for less than 50% of work in the residence country under conditions). Official SVB: https://www.svb.nl/en/id/you-work-in-2-or-more-countries/cross-border-teleworking
- A temporary assignment with an A1 can have different rules; check actual status with HR/SVB/Spanish TGSS.
- AEAT considers Spanish tax residency under, inter alia, >183 days in Spain during the tax year or centre of economic interests: https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/en_gb/no-residentes/residencia-personas-fisicas-juridicas/persona-fisica-residente-espana.html
- Ask each employer: **Can I work permanently and be paid compliantly while living in Spain? Do you have Spanish payroll/entity or EOR? What are the travel obligations and location eligibility?** Never assume “remote EMEA” means “remote from Spain.”

## 10-day application sequence
Days 1–2: Finalise two job-specific CVs, validate links and review PR #3/52; build 3 simple SENDA/industrial customer stories (problem, action, test, limitation).
Days 3–5: Apply first to Phrase and EGA; investigate MaintainX Barcelona variant; start 10–15 employer-led relevant applications, *not* random high-volume spam.
Days 6–10: Conduct mock interviews; practise API/webhook examples, Excel migration (XLOOKUP/pivots), customer triage and AI workflow measurement; follow up on submitted applications.
Weekly: Verify every posting on the employer board and record timestamp, city, salary, missing skills and application status. No auto-application without final human review.
